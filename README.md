# TWIN GLADE

A cozy spot-the-difference game. Two near-identical hand-drawn scenes sit side by side;
find all 7 differences in each of three original scenes.

## The game

- **Level 1 — Meadow Morning**: a cottage, a big oak, sun, clouds, flowers, a butterfly, a pond, a fence.
- **Level 2 — Harbour Dusk**: boats, a lighthouse, gulls, crates, lanterns, a cat on the dock, a flag.
- **Level 3 — Starlit Camp**: a tent, campfire, pine trees, moon, an owl, a backpack, marshmallow sticks.

Each scene is drawn from scratch in vector canvas art — no image files, no copied art. The two
pictures are drawn by the same scene function with a per-side difference mask, so they are truly
near-identical except for the 7 hidden spots.

## How to play

Click a difference with the mouse, or move the crosshair with the arrow keys / WASD and press
**Enter** or **Space** to mark it. A correct find rings a green marker on both pictures and plays a
chime. A wrong click flashes a red X, plays a low buzz and adds 5 seconds to the timer — no
lives lost, stay cozy.

- **H** — hint (3 per level): pulses an unfound difference for 2 seconds.
- **P** / **Esc** — pause / resume.
- **R** — restart the level (or play again on the final screen).
- **M** — mute.
